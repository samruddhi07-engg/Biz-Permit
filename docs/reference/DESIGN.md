## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, editorial-grade civic architecture engineered for enterprise-scale permitting, compliance, and regulatory affairs. It merges the gravitas of institutional governance with the immaculate precision of luxury editorial design.

### Design Aesthetic: Monochromatic Precision Minimalism
The style relies entirely on strict grayscale contrast, high-order geometric structure, and pristine negative space. Chromatic cues are intentionally rejected. Authority, progress, urgency, and verification are communicated through typographic weight, micro-borders, deep obsidian solid planes, and luminous porcelain surfaces.

### Emotional Response & Principles
- **Sovereign Trust:** Every viewport conveys total stability and regulatory finality.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts treat whitespace as structural matter, avoiding clutter, noisy gradients, or decorative embellishments.
- **Enterprise Velocity:** High-density forms and data-heavy tables remain frictionless through high contrast, optical alignment, and predictable pill-form affordances.

## Colors

The design system implements a strict zero-chroma palette composed of obsidian blacks, cool-cast architectural zincs, porcelain foundation tones, and pure white light.

### Color Tokens & Assignments
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white used as the foundational canvas to produce maximum luminous contrast.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#F9FAFB`):** Luminous off-white / porcelain for large inset containers, form backgrounds, and side-rail navigation.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#F4F4F5`):** Subtly deeper tone for nested cards, interactive table headers, and structural divides.
- **Border Subtle (`#EEEEF0`):** Hairline edge boundary for low-contrast interior framing.
- **Border Default (`#E4E4E7`):** Primary boundary metric for inputs, card perimeters, and modular separators.
- **Text & Foreground Primary (`#0A0A0A`):** Deep obsidian black for primary headings, body copy, and maximum-authority elements.
- **Text Secondary (`#71717A`):** Balanced zinc charcoal for metadata, label caps, helper text, and secondary timestamps.
- **Interactive Solid (`#111111`):** Dense carbon for primary buttons and high-priority status indicators.

### Strict Monochromatic Rules
- No semantic colors (green, amber, red, blue) are permitted across the core UI.
- Success, warning, and failure states are expressed through typographic descriptors, custom line icons, tonal contrast shifts, and obsidian pill indicators.

## Typography

The type system is built on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, calibrated specifically for civic enterprise tools that demand both rapid scanning and high-end editorial polish.

### Type Rules & Hierarchy
- **Tracking Discipline:** Larger headline sizes use negative letter-spacing (`-0.035em` down to `-0.02em`) to compact geometric letterforms into clean, unified blocks. Micro-metadata and uppercase captions (`label-caps`) employ expansive tracking (`0.08em`) for immediate legal and structural recognition.
- **Contrast Ratios:** Pure obsidian (`#0A0A0A`) is preserved for titles and key data strings. Secondary zinc (`#71717A`) is strictly applied to contextual labels, column keys, and supporting explanations.
- **Tabular Data:** All financial figures, permit serial numbers, and regulatory timestamps must leverage open-type tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) to preserve vertical alignment across data grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a mathematical 8pt spacing grid combined with a structured 12-column fluid grid system.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 40px (`2.5rem`) canvas margins. Max container constraint: 1440px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) outer margin.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) outer margin.

### Structural Spacing Directives
- **Rhythm Consistency:** Margins between major form steps or distinct section cards use `space-xl` (40px) or `space-2xl` (64px) to preserve institutional breathing room.
- **Component Enclosures:** Form fields, table rows, and list items default to `space-md` (16px) or `space-sm` (8px) internal padding, ensuring tight scanning metrics without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids decorative drop shadows and vibrant blurs. Visual depth is achieved through **calibrated tonal stacking** and **crisp 1px micro-borders**.

### Surface Stacking Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Layer 1 (Modular Insets & Section Backgrounds):** Off-White / Porcelain (`#F9FAFB`) bounded by a 1px border (`#EEEEF0`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Cards & Popovers):** Solid Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) elevated solely via a precise 1px border (`#E4E4E7`) and an ambient, ultra-subtle shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Overlays & Enterprise Modals):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) framed by `#0A0A0A` borders with a solid backdrop veil composed of `#0A0A0A` at 40% opacity.

## Shapes

The interface balances architectural rectilinear framing with modern pill-shaped interactive touchpoints.

### Shape Geometry System
- **Pill Primitives (Full Round / 9999px):** All actionable buttons, status chips, search bars, and filter toggles feature complete pill geometry (`rounded-full`). This provides humanized contrast against orthogonal data grids.
- **Structured Containers (Roundedness Level 3):** Modal windows, data tables, and major document containers utilize an outer boundary curvature of `1rem` (16px) with an interior card curvature of `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Control Elements:** Small controls (checkboxes) use a tailored micro-radius of `0.25rem` (4px) to preserve crisp legibility at small scales.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`), solid Obsidian (`#0A0A0A`) fill, pure white (`#FFFFFF`) typography. Hover state transitions to `#27272A` with a subtle transform scale (`scale-98`). Height: 44px (Standard), 36px (Compact).
- **Secondary:** Porcelain fill (`#F4F4F5`), Obsidian (`#0A0A0A`) text, 1px border (`#E4E4E7`). Hover state darkens fill to `#E4E4E7`.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px Obsidian (`#0A0A0A`) border, Obsidian text.
- **Ghost:** Transparent fill, Zinc (`#71717A`) text. Hover introduces `#F4F4F5` background and `#0A0A0A` text.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict pill silhouette (`rounded-full`) with a height of 24px and padding of `0 12px`.
- **Verified / Approved:** Solid Obsidian (`#0A0A0A`) background with White (`#FFFFFF`) text, prefixed with a monochrome dot.
- **Pending / In Review:** Porcelain (`#F4F4F5`) background, `#0A0A0A` text, 1px `#E4E4E7` border.
- **Action Required / Rejected:** White (`#FFFFFF`) background, `#0A0A0A` text, 1.5px solid `#0A0A0A` border with an interior strikethrough or alert glyph.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Height: 48px. Pill or soft-stadium radius (`9999px` for standard single-lines, `12px` for multiline textareas).
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px `#E4E4E7`. Text: `#0A0A0A`. Placeholder: `#A1A1AA`.
- Focus state: Replaces border with a solid 1.5px `#0A0A0A` stroke and zero chromatic ring glow.
- Helper labels: Positioned above in `label-caps` typography utilizing `#71717A`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px square with `4px` corner radius. Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E4E4E7` border. Checked: `#0A0A0A` fill with an ultra-fine white checkmark icon.
- **Radios:** 18x18px circle. Checked: Obsidian outer ring enclosing an obsidian center point on pure white.

### Cards & Document Containers
- Dual-tier structure: Outermost frame uses `#F9FAFB` with a 1px `#EEEEF0` border.
- Inner card bodies use `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E4E4E7` border, containing permit specifications, tracking data, or enterprise metadata.
- Padding follows `space-lg` (24px) internally.

### Tables & Enterprise Data Grids
- Flat, seamless borders. Table header features `#F4F4F5` fill with `label-caps` styling in `#71717A`.
- Row height: 56px with a bottom hairline border (`1px solid #EEEEF0`).
- Hover row state shifts to `#F9FAFB` smoothly over 150ms.
- Permit numbers, routing keys, and timestamps use tabular numerals in Obsidian (`#0A0A0A`).
