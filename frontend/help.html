What documents are mandatory for a General Trade License?

Every commercial trade application requires a valid lease agreement or property title deed, government-issued photo identification for the primary business owner, and proof of commercial registered address.

Which file formats and sizes are permitted for document uploads?

The portal accepts PDF, JPG, JPEG, and PNG files up to 10MB per document. Files are encrypted on disk and checked for SHA-256 cryptographic integrity upon upload.

What happens if an officer requests a correction?

Your application status transitions to Action Required. You will see the specific officer notes on your application details page. You can simply upload a corrected document or modify your form responses and click Resubmit without paying any additional fees.

How is an on-site inspection coordinated?

When physical premises examination is mandated, a municipal inspector is assigned. A scheduled inspection date appears in your application timeline. The inspector conducts the field review and uploads a digital checklist with outcome verification.

How does license renewal work?

Active permits nearing expiration (within 60–90 days) feature a single-click Renew button in your My Licenses tab. This initiates a linked renewal application prefilled with your existing records.
