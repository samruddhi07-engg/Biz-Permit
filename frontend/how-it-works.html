How BizPermit Works

A transparent, end-to-end municipal licensing lifecycle engineered across 4 dedicated database engines to guarantee audited SLAs, verified compliance, and zero lost paperwork.

1

Discover Applicable Licenses

Select your commercial activity sector. Our Neo4j Graph Engine traverses municipal rules to instantly determine every required trade credential, zoning permit, and prerequisite dependency.

Powered by Neo4j Knowledge Graph
2

Prepare Dynamic Filing

Fill in specialized commercial details tailored to your exact permit (e.g. kitchen extraction hoods, chemical manifests, or pharmacy cold-storage). Flexible data schemas are stored safely in MongoDB.

Dynamic Schemas in MongoDB
3

Submit Supporting Evidence

Upload lease agreements, architectural floor blueprints, and operator credentials. The server computes SHA-256 cryptographic hashes and validates MIME types before admitting filings.

Secure Vault Storage & Hash Audits
4

Licensing Officer Adjudication

Authoritative departmental examiners verify documents item-by-item. If an omission is discovered, a transparent correction request is flagged without discarding your existing progress.

Audited Workflow in MySQL
5

On-Site Regulatory Inspection

For high-occupancy or health-critical businesses, certified municipal field inspectors conduct physical examinations, completing structured digital checklists and recording photo evidence directly on-site.

Checklist & Photo Metadata in MongoDB
6

Statutory Approval or Rejection

The presiding municipal director evaluates verification logs and inspection outcomes, registering formal legal findings into the system audit trail.

Immutable Status Logs
7

Digital Permit Issuance & QR Certificate

Upon approval, an official license number and high-resolution certificate with cryptographic QR code are created transactionally. Anyone can scan the QR code to verify validity instantly.

Public Verification Enabled
8

Automated Renewal Cycles

Proactive alerts trigger 90 and 30 days prior to license expiration. Business owners can initiate a 1-click renewal application prefilled with previous records.

Zero-Downtime Compliance
